About World

The realm of Candeliques, Purgatory

Ground is a mix of dirt and soot

The “animals” that inhabit the land are called Castings, shadow figures with glowing white eyes. They exhibit hostile behavior normally but sometimes they are drawn to certain Candelique’s with a strong flame and become almost docile.

There are faint whispers that can be heard the further from an urban area or city you are, they grow louder the darker the area is and the less populated it is. The cause of this is the Casting trying to lure people out into the open to take their flame. They are known to mimic people's voices with surprising accuracy.

The Purgatory that the Candeliques live in has no known end to it as it seems to stretch on forever with jagged mountains scattered across the terrain.

The trees and all vegetation that grows in Purgatory is a deep soot black and extremely brittle making it hard to grow anything from being covered in soot and ash in the environment for so long, yet they still produce leaves and fruit that become covered in soot quickly. Fruit that is harvested quickly and protected from the soot is a very rare delicacy that Candeliques love.

The night sky shifts between black and purple, casting ominous shadows everywhere.

The sun is pure white, illuminating the land making the pitch black ground appear to be a light gray with how intense the sun is.

The air is always filled with smoke meaning humans are unable to breathe the air but Candeliques have grown used to the smoke filled air.

The cities and towns that are scattered across Purgatory are almost a perfectly mirrored image of the cities and towns of the real world from before death. At night a thick fog surrounds all cities and towns where light isn’t shown making it almost impossible to navigate without a light source.
